A

Enter your name above to start.
  • Molecules hop back and forth over a hill. How far downhill the products sit, and how tall the hill is, are two different questions.
  • Drag the products' level and the top of the hill, then Go. Hit each target.

The hillLeft valley: reactants. Right valley: products. Up: free energy (kJ/mol). Each dot is a molecule.Drag near the right valley to move the products up or down; drag near the top to raise or lower the hill. ΔG: products minus reactants. The hill's height is measured from the reactants.Molecules hop both ways, uphill hops more rarely. The enzyme toggle lowers the hill (dashed); it moves neither valley.

B

Finish Stage A to unlock this section.
  • A cell has to make plenty of uphill reactions go. It pays for them by running a downhill one at the same time.
  • Add reactions from the shelf to the one you need until the whole thing goes. Click a bar to take it off.

The billFirst bar: the reaction you need, its ΔG (kJ/mol). Each bar after it: a reaction run at the same time. The black line: the running total. The coupled reaction goes if the total ends below zero.Click a bar (not the first) to remove it.

TargetFour targets in any order. Use as few shelf reactions as will do the job.
